# Awesome Baby Tracking Apps

A curated list of iPhone apps for logging a baby's feeds, sleep, diapers and growth. Each note says who the app suits, based on its App Store listing.

This list is maintained by Ken (Kennily), the developer of Deerly – Baby Tracker & Log. Entries marked (ours) are our own apps; everything else is listed on its merits.

> **Not medical advice.** These apps help you keep a log. Ask your pediatrician about your baby's health, feeding, sleep and growth.

## Feeding & sleep logs

- [Baby Tracker - Newborn Log](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id779656557) - Long-running tracker by Nighp for feeds, pumping, sleep and diapers; core tracking is free and a Baby Tracker Plus subscription adds on-device timing cues.
- [Huckleberry: Baby Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1169136078) - Suits parents who mainly want sleep guidance and expert parenting advice alongside tracking; Plus and Premium memberships add sleep predictions and AI guidance.
- [Baby Tracker & Newborn Log](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id551448817) - Sprout Baby Tracker by Med ART Studios, an all-in-one log for feeds, sleep, diapers and milestones with auto-renewing subscription options.
- [Baby Tracker • My Baby](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1439575933) - Straightforward log for sleep, breastfeeding, pumping, diapers and health notes such as temperature and vaccinations.
- [Nara Baby & Pregnancy Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1444639029) - Calm-looking tracker for naps, wake windows, feeds and diapers that also handles twins and caregiver sharing.

## Shared family logging

- [PiyoLog: Shared Baby Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1252857347) - Suits parents who like a Japanese-style interface with real-time sharing between partners and a babysitter companion app.
- [Baby Connect: Newborn Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id326574411) - Cross-platform tracker that also runs in a web browser for sharing with nannies and daycare; saving entries needs a subscription after the free trial.
- [Deerly – Baby Tracker & Log](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6802015210) - Suits families who want voice logging and free real-time family logging, plus Home Screen widgets and an Apple Watch nursing timer; English and Traditional Chinese only. ## Growth, milestones & memories

- [Glow Baby Tracker & Growth App](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id1077177456) - Suits parents who want AI-generated insights and broader parenting content; free to use with an optional Glow Premium subscription.
- [Hatch Grow](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id976334466) - Companion app for Hatch's smart changing pad with a built-in scale, and can also be used on its own to log feeds, diapers and sleep.
- [TimeHut - Baby Album](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id565951606) - Photo and video timeline of a child's growth to share with family, with a paid storage subscription.
- [BabyCenter Pregnancy Tracker](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id386022579) - Week-by-week pregnancy and parenting content from BabyCenter, for parents who want reading material more than a detailed log.
